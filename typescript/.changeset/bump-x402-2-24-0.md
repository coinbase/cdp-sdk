Bumped the `@x402/core`, `@x402/evm`, `@x402/extensions`, and `@x402/svm` peer dependencies to `^2.24.0`, and expanded `CdpX402Client`/`createX402Server` scheme coverage: `CdpX402Client` now supports the `upto` scheme on Solana by default (previously EVM-only) and registers the `authCapture` scheme by default on Base, alongside `exact` and `upto`. `createX402Server`'s `upto` support remains EVM-only for now.

Also reworked `createX402Server`'s route config surface: the simplified shape (`price` + optional fields) is now named `CdpSimplifiedRouteConfig`, and `CdpRouteConfig` — the type accepted by `createX402Server`'s `routes` map — is a union of `CdpSimplifiedRouteConfig | RouteConfig` (the full x402 format, unchanged). Simplified routes gained an optional `paymentFlow` shorthand (`"authorization"` | `"upfront"`, exact-scheme only), applied to every network the route expands to.
