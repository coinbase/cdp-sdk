Bumped the `@x402/core`, `@x402/evm`, `@x402/extensions`, and `@x402/svm` peer dependencies to `^2.24.0`, and expanded `CdpX402Client`/`createX402Server` scheme coverage: the `upto` scheme is now supported on Solana by default for both the client and server (previously EVM-only), and `CdpX402Client` now registers the `authCapture` scheme by default on Base, alongside `exact` and `upto`. Also added `CdpSimplifiedRouteConfig` (the simplified route shape previously named `CdpRouteConfig`) and widened `CdpRouteConfig` into a union of `CdpSimplifiedRouteConfig | CdpX402RouteConfig` — the type accepted by `createX402Server`'s `routes` map.
